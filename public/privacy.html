Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Cardello ("Cardello," "we," "us," or "our") collects when you use our website and place an order, how we use it, and who we share it with. By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Cardello:

Information you give us directly: the photo(s) you upload, the recipient's name and details you provide, the card message you write, your email address, and the shipping address you enter at checkout.

Payment information: when you check out, your payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, our payment processor. Cardello does not see or store your full card number.

Automatically collected information: basic technical data like your browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected through standard website analytics and (where enabled) advertising pixels such as the Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel, used to measure how our ads are performing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

• Generate your personalized card design using AI image-generation technology
• Process your payment and fulfill your order (printing and mailing the card)
• Send you order confirmations and updates about your order
• Improve our website and card designs
• Measure and improve the performance of our advertising, where you have not opted out of tracking

3. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Cardello, and only as needed to provide the service:

• Stripe — to process payments securely
• OpenAI — to generate your AI card design from your photo and details
• Vercel — to host our website and store your generated card design image
• Resend — to send order confirmation emails
• Meta and TikTok — for advertising and ad performance measurement, where applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Photos You Upload

Photos you upload are used solely to generate your card design and are shared with our AI image-generation provider (OpenAI) for that purpose. The generated design is stored so your order can be fulfilled and so you can reference it if you contact us about your order.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including advertising pixels from Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the performance of our ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings; note that disabling cookies may affect how the site functions.

6. Data Retention

We keep order information, including the card design and message, for as long as needed to fulfill your order and to handle any follow-up questions or issues, and as required for our business and tax records. You can ask us to delete your information by contacting us, subject to any records we're required to keep by law.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion, by reaching out through our Contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

Cardello is not intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is used? Reach out through our Contact page.
