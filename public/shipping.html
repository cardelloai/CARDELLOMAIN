Shipping & Returns

Where your card goes, how fast it gets there, and what happens if something's wrong.

Shipping

We currently ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. Once your design is finalized and your order is placed, your card is printed and shipped within a few business days. You'll receive a confirmation once it's on its way.

Card sizes

  • Standard (5x7") — our most popular size, fits standard envelopes
  • Large (7x10") — extra room to make a bigger impression

Returns & reprints

Every card is custom-made just for you, so we're not able to accept general returns. That said, if your card arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you approved, contact us within 14 days of delivery and we'll reprint or refund your order.

Questions about an order

Reach out any time on our Contact page and we'll help sort it out.
