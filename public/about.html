About Cardello

A card that actually looks like the person you're sending it to.

Why we started Cardello

Picking out a greeting card usually means standing in an aisle, reading twenty cards that don't quite say what you mean, and settling for "close enough." We thought there was a better way — one that starts with the actual person you're celebrating, not a rack of generic designs.

How it works

You tell us a little about who the card is for and what makes them special. You upload one photo of them. Our AI turns that photo into a fun, custom illustration — your dad riding a reindeer, your grandma in her garden, your dog at his own birthday party — and builds it into a real greeting card. You pick your favorite design, add your message, and we print it on real cardstock and mail it for you.

Who Cardello is for

Cardello was built to be simple enough for anyone — big buttons, plain language, and one easy question at a time, whether you're shopping from your phone or your computer.

AI-personalized greeting cards for any occasion — designed from your photo, printed, and mailed for you.
