Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How long does it take to get my card?

Your AI designs are ready in about 20–30 seconds. Once you place your order, your card is printed and shipped within a few business days.

What photo should I use?

A clear, well-lit photo where the person's face is visible works best. One photo is all we need.

Will the card actually look like the person?

Our AI keeps their real likeness — face shape, hair, expression — while turning it into a fun, illustrated style. It's a stylized cartoon, not a photo, but it should still be clearly recognizable.

Can I see the card before I pay?

Yes. You'll see your finished design and can edit your message before you're asked for any payment information.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship within the United States and Canada. See our Shipping & Returns page for details.

What if I'm not happy with my card?

Reach out to us on the Contact page and we'll make it right.

Is my photo kept private?

Your photo is used only to create your card design and isn't shared or sold. It's stored only as long as needed to complete and print your order.
